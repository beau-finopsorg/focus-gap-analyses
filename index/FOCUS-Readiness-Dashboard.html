FOCUS readiness benchmark

FOCUS data generator readiness

A red-amber-green scorecard across the 73 providers we have analyzed. AWS and Nebius are certified under the FinOps Foundation's FinOps Certified FOCUS Conformant program; another 11 are Supported (they have given the Foundation a FOCUS export), listed on focus.finops.org/get-started and tagged with the FOCUS version each ships. Every other provider is rated on five readiness dimensions, showing how close it is to producing a FOCUS export. Linode and Microsoft appear at multiple billing surfaces.

At a glance

Where the field stands

The official pipeline runs Supported, then a Conformance Gap Report, then FinOps Certified FOCUS Conformant. AWS and Nebius have completed it and are certified. Eleven more are Supported: they have given the FinOps Foundation a FOCUS export and are listed on the get-started page. Vercel, Databricks, and Oracle Cloud ship v1.3; AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Nebius, Grafana, and Redis ship v1.2; Alibaba, Tencent, Huawei, and OVHcloud ship v1.0. Being Supported is not the same as being certified under that program, and it is not a rating from this unofficial community report. Every other provider on the board is rated by our readiness rubric to show how far it is from producing a FOCUS export.

The rubric

Five dimensions, rolled up to a readiness band

Each provider is rated Strong, Partial, or Gap on five dimensions, taken from its column-by-column gap analysis. Cost and coverage carry the most weight, FOCUS shape and granularity next, allocation last.

Cost

Are List, Billed, Effective, and Contracted cost present, or only list price?

Granularity

Daily and sub-monthly charge records, or monthly aggregates?

Allocation

Resource identity, tags, and sub-account or cost-center attribution.

Coverage

Share of applicable Mandatory and Conditional columns populated.

FOCUS shape

FOCUS column IDs, key-value tags, valid enums, correct types.

How to read it

Patterns worth noting

The common shape is "data exists, not yet FOCUS-shaped." Most Structured providers are green on cost, granularity, and coverage but amber on FOCUS shape: they emit usage data with the right substance under native field names, and need a transform layer, not new instrumentation.

SaaS and license vendors cluster in Emerging. Enterprise and subscription billers (Adobe, Salesforce, SAP, ServiceNow, VMware, Workday, Nutanix) tend to be monthly or annual, with list and contracted pricing off-system and little resource or tag attribution, so they share a Partial-and-Gap profile.

The few Near-ready providers already emit FOCUS-aligned output. Vercel and IBM Cloud expose native FOCUS-shaped data; GitHub has the full cost trio and rich allocation and needs only the shaping step.

Allocation is the most common weak spot. Tags rarely propagate into billing data, so even strong providers sit at Partial on allocation.

Scores are derived from the FOCUS gap analyses for each provider and reflect the data sources reviewed, not formal FinOps Foundation certification results. Ratings follow a fixed five-dimension rubric applied consistently across providers. AWS is shown as a certified-leader anchor.
