# Disclaimer

This is the canonical disclaimer for this repo and every report in it.

## Unofficial

These reports are a personal and community project by Beau Nelford, FOCUS Technical Community Architect, acting in a personal and community capacity. They are not official FOCUS, FinOps Foundation, or Linux Foundation publications. They do not state a project position and do not endorse any vendor.

## Public documentation only

Each report is a reading of public documentation. It does not use private access, vendor interviews, or live testing. A vendor may support more than it has published, or less than it has claimed.

## Dated

Each report shows an "Analysis run" date. It describes what public documentation showed on that date. Products and documentation change. A report may be out of date.

## Not a certification

Ratings (Aligned, Partially aligned, Not yet aligned (per public docs)) are not a certification result, a conformance verdict, or an official project verdict. They are a reading of public docs and may be incomplete.

## Marks

Product names, company names, and marks belong to their owners. Their use here is only to say which product a report covers.

## Specification

The FinOps Open Cost and Usage Specification (FOCUS) is published at https://focus.finops.org. This repo does not host or change it. For feedback on the specification itself, use https://focus.finops.org/feedback/.

## How to request a correction

Vendors and others can correct a report. [Open a vendor correction issue](https://github.com/beau-finopsorg/focus-gap-analyses/issues/new?template=vendor-correction.yml) or send a pull request, and cite public evidence. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

The written analyses are under CC BY 4.0, which includes its own disclaimer of warranties. Do not rely on a report for a purchasing, legal, or compliance decision without checking the vendor's current documentation.
